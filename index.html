ZTech Lead Generation

ZTech Lead Generation is a Windows desktop application by Zunitech. It helps one operator research business prospects, draft an outreach email for each one, review and approve it by hand, and send it from the operator's own Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox.

How it uses Google

When you connect a Gmail mailbox, ZTech asks Google for two permissions:

  • gmail.send: to send an email you approved, one message at a time, and the follow-ups you activated, from your mailbox.
  • gmail.metadata: to read the headers (sender, subject, reply references) of messages in your mailbox so it can recognise a reply or an unsubscribe request to an email it sent. It never reads message bodies or attachments.

Everything ZTech stores runs and stays on your own computer. Read the Privacy Policy for the details and the Terms of Service for the conditions of use.
